# Apple HIG: Writing

Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing (read 2026-11-25)

- Decide the app's voice. Keep a list of common terms so language stays consistent.
- Match tone to the context and what people are doing.
- "If you can use fewer words, do so. When in doubt, read your writing out loud."
- Use possessive pronouns sparingly: "Favorites" beats "Your Favorites".
- Buttons and links: "it’s almost always best to use a verb". "Send" beats "Let’s do it!". Avoid "Click here".
- Errors: show them "as close to the problem as possible, avoid blame, and be clear about what someone can do to fix it".
  Example: "Choose a password with at least 8 characters" beats "That password is too short".
  Skip "oops!" and robotic messages like "Invalid name."
- Empty states: give clear next steps and a button or link to act. Do not put crucial info there; it disappears.
- Pick one capitalization style per element type and use it everywhere.
