# Worked example: copy deck (before → after)

| Where | Weak | Good | Rule (HIG Writing / Alerts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Network error | "Oops! Something went wrong." | "Couldn't get questions. Check your connection and try again." | No "oops"; say what to do |
| Rate limit | "Error 5" | "Getting questions… this can take a few seconds." | Not "Error"; plain words |
| Empty history | (blank) | "No scores yet. Pick a topic to play your first round." + **Pick a Topic** | Empty states give next steps |
| Login fail | "Invalid credentials." | "That password didn't work. Try again." | No robotic messages |
| Approve button | "OK" | "Commit 3 Files" | Verbs that describe the result; avoid OK |
| Cancel button | "No" | "Cancel" | Always "Cancel" |
| Results button | "Let's go again!" | "Play Again" | Clear beats clever |
| Lockout | "Access denied." | "Too many tries. Try again in 15 minutes." | Say what happened and when it ends |
